About this episode
Jonah Wells and Sarah Kim break down the complete production workflow behind Season 3 — from room treatment to final master. They cover dynamic mic technique, compression philosophy, and spatial layering. If you've ever wondered how to make a podcast sound like a studio record, this is the episode for you. Recorded live at the Sound & Vision conference in Chicago.
